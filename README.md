## Rubikon Venture

**A personal digital ecosystem for agentic collaboration — and a living collaboration infrastructure.**

Rubikon Venture is an experimental system for understanding **who you trust, how you collaborate, what people are building, and where the right person can be connected to the right project at the right moment**.

Instead of treating a professional network as a static list of contacts, Rubikon imagines it as a **living ecosystem** that changes with conversations, shared work, cultural fit, reliability, interests, and evolving trust.

The current prototype is organized around three layers:

* **Identity** — what someone is building, how they collaborate, and what they will not compromise.
* **Connection Map** — a fluid map of the relationships that matter most, beginning with a Tier 1 network of high-context trusted connections.
* **FlowBoard** — a live relevance layer for projects, capabilities, people, opportunities, and possible next actions.

The longer-term vision is to connect Rubikon with **agentic systems capable of listening, remembering, understanding context, and acting across interactions**. As conversations and projects evolve, the system could continuously update relationship context, surface relevant collaborators, identify talent-project fit, and suggest timely introductions.

Rubikon could eventually extend beyond a website into a browser extension, mobile application, smartwatch interface, or wearable layer.

The underlying idea remains the same:

> **Your network is not a contact list. It is living infrastructure.**
